Privacy Policy

Locus

Last updated: August 29, 2026

We operate Locus, a field ticket and map workflow platform for excavation and utility locate operations. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This policy covers the Locus web app, related websites, and services we provide to your organization. It is written for people using Locus at work. It is not legal advice.

Information we collect

Depending on how you and your organization use Locus, we may collect:

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, username, organization, role, and language preference.
  • Sign-in data — the password you create (stored as a one-way hash; we do not store it in plain text) and session tokens so you can stay signed in.
  • Work data — tickets, job details, addresses, maps and drawings, notes, photos, documents, damage or incident reports, and other content you or your teammates enter.
  • People on tickets — contact names and phone numbers that appear on locate tickets (for example, a job's principal contact).
  • Location — we do not collect your location all the time, and we do not save a location trail from the map. If location is on so you can see yourself on the map (for example, follow-me), that is only used on your device to show the map. We collect a location point only at specific moments, such as when you take a photo or create a field note, so that item can be placed on the job. You can turn location off in your browser or device settings.
  • Connected locate accounts — if your organization links a utility locate system (such as USAN / 811), we store those credentials in encrypted form so Locus can sync tickets on your behalf.
  • Technical data — browser and device type, IP address, and service logs we need to run, secure, and fix the platform.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide Locus to you and your organization, including maps, tickets, photos, and documents.
  • Sign you in and control who can see what.
  • Sync tickets and related data with locate systems your organization connects.
  • Send service emails, such as account verification and password reset messages.
  • Keep the service secure, debug problems, and meet legal requirements.

We do not use your information to show third-party ads, and we do not sell personal information.

Who can see your data

Locus is an organization product. People in your organization with the right role can see work data for that organization — including tickets, photos, and documents — according to the permissions your admins set.

If someone in your organization creates a share link for a project, anyone with that link can view the shared map, photos, and related job information until the link is turned off.

On your device

Locus keeps some information in your browser so the app can work, including sign-in tokens, preferences such as "Remember me," and (when you use Locus as an installed or offline-capable app) cached pages, map tiles, tickets, and photos. Signing out removes session tokens from that browser. Clearing site data or uninstalling the app removes local copies on that device. Copies stored for your organization on our servers are not deleted just because you sign out.

Companies that help us run Locus

We use other companies to operate the service. They process information only as needed to provide their part of it:

  • Hosting and storage — Cloudflare hosts the app, database, file storage (photos and documents), and delivers pages. Service email is sent through that infrastructure.
  • Maps — OpenStreetMap, and Mapbox when your environment is configured for it, to show basemaps. Using those layers can send map requests (and typically your IP address) to those providers.
  • Utility locate systems — such as USAN / 811, when your organization connects those accounts.
  • Fonts — some sign-in and legal pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

Those companies have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to read them if you use those features.

How we share information

We share information only when needed to run Locus, including:

  • With your organization and authorized teammates.
  • With the service providers listed above, under contracts that limit how they can use it.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • If we were to transfer the business, in which case this policy would still apply until you are notified of a change.

How long we keep information

We keep account and work data for as long as your organization uses Locus, and as long as we need it to provide the service, settle disputes, enforce our terms, or meet legal requirements (including locate-ticket records your organization may need to retain). You or an organization admin can ask us to delete an account. We may keep some records when the law or your organization's obligations require it.

Security

We use measures designed to protect information, including HTTPS, access controls, hashed passwords, and encrypted storage of connected locate-account passwords. No online service is completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

Your choices

  • Update profile details in the app where your role allows it.
  • Turn off browser or device location permission to stop the map from reading your position and to skip attaching a location to photos or field notes.
  • Sign out to remove session tokens from that device.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information. We will honor requests as required by law and as allowed by your organization's settings (your employer may need to approve deletion of work records).

If you are in a place with additional privacy rights (for example, California), you can contact us to exercise those rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

Locus is for business use. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above when we do. If a change is significant, we will try to notify you by email or in the app. Please check this page for the current version.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or our data practices? Email your deployment operator (configure support contact in Worker env — see docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
